How much is a Phantasmal Flames master set?

A Phantasmal Flames master set is 214 cards, $1,029.23 at today's prices (TCGPlayer market prices via TCGCSV as of October 15, 2026): base (main set 1–94) 94 cards $23.32, reverse holos 84 cards $20.30, secret rares 36 cards $985.61.

A master set is one copy of every card in the set, including the secret rares numbered past the printed set size, plus the reverse holo version of every card that has one. In Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution sets the ex, Ultra Rare and other higher-rarity cards don't come in reverse holo, so the reverse holo count (84) is lower than the main set count (94). Our master set price is the full set plus every reverse holo, at TCGPlayer market prices via TCGCSV. Some collectors also count promos or stamped versions. We don't include them.

Phantasmal Flames FAQ

How much does it cost to complete Phantasmal Flames?

How many cards are in Phantasmal Flames?

Phantasmal Flames has 130 cards: 94 in the main set (numbered 1–94) plus 36 numbered above 94.

What is the most expensive card in Phantasmal Flames?

How much does it cost to master set Phantasmal Flames?

214 cards, about $1,029.23 at today's prices (TCGPlayer market prices via TCGCSV as of October 15, 2026): base (main set 1–94) 94 cards $23.32, reverse holos 84 cards $20.30, secret rares 36 cards $985.61. Most of it is the 36 secret rares, which add $985.61.

How many cards are in a Phantasmal Flames master set?

214: 94 main-set cards, 36 secret rares and 84 reverse holos. 10 main-set cards have no reverse holo.

What counts toward a Phantasmal Flames master set?

Every card numbered in the set, including secret rares past #94, plus the reverse holo of every card that has one. Promos and stamped versions aren't included in our total.

What's the cheapest way to master set Phantasmal Flames?
