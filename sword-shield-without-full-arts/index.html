FAQ

What is the cheapest Sword & Shield set to complete?

At TCGPlayer market prices as of October 23, 2026, the cheapest Sword & Shield set to finish without its full arts is Shining Fates at $36.06, followed by Battle Styles ($49.78) and Lost Origin ($58.87). Shining Fates is a smaller special set; the cheapest main expansion is Battle Styles at $49.78 for cards 1–141.

What does "without full arts" mean?

It means cards 1 up to the last number before the first Full Art, alt-art, Rainbow or Secret card inside the printed set total (by the card data's rarity). In Sword & Shield sets those cards are numbered inside the main set, so for example Evolving Skies is $210.68 for cards 1–165 but $2,806.57 for the whole 203-card main set.

How much do the full arts add?

The most in Evolving Skies: its full arts and other cards after #165 add $2,595.89 to the main set ($210.68 without them, $2,806.57 with them), at TCGPlayer market prices as of October 23, 2026.

Which Sword & Shield set costs the most even without full arts?

Evolving Skies, at $210.68 for cards 1–165 (TCGPlayer market prices, October 23, 2026).
