Shining Fates is a smaller special set; the cheapest main expansion is Battle Styles at $49.69 for cards 1–141.

FAQ

What is the cheapest Sword & Shield set to complete?

At TCGPlayer market prices as of October 15, 2026, the cheapest Sword & Shield set to finish without its full arts is Shining Fates at $34.62, followed by Battle Styles ($49.69) and Lost Origin ($57.72). Shining Fates is a smaller special set; the cheapest main expansion is Battle Styles at $49.69 for cards 1–141.

What does "without full arts" mean?

It means cards 1 up to the last number before the first Full Art, alt-art, Rainbow or Secret card inside the printed set total (by the card data's rarity). In Sword & Shield sets those cards are numbered inside the main set, so for example Evolving Skies is $203.54 for cards 1–165 but $2,880.16 for the whole 203-card main set.

How much do the full arts add?

The most in Evolving Skies: its full arts and other cards after #165 add $2,676.62 to the main set ($203.54 without them, $2,880.16 with them), at TCGPlayer market prices as of October 15, 2026.

Which Sword & Shield set costs the most even without full arts?

Evolving Skies, at $203.54 for cards 1–165 (TCGPlayer market prices, October 15, 2026).
