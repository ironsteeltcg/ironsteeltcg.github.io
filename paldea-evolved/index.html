Paldea Evolved FAQ

How much does it cost to complete Paldea Evolved?

About $1,813.88 for one copy of all 279 cards at TCGPlayer market prices. The main set (cards 1–193) is about $43.71. A master set with all 176 reverse holos is about $1,855.18.

How many cards are in Paldea Evolved?

Paldea Evolved has 279 cards: 193 in the main set (numbered 1–193) plus 86 numbered above 193.

What is the most expensive card in Paldea Evolved?

The most expensive Paldea Evolved card is Magikarp (#203, Illustration Rare) at about $352.72 market price.
