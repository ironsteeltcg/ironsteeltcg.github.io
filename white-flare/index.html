How much is a White Flare master set?

A White Flare master set is 253 cards, $2,925.10 at today's prices (TCGPlayer market prices via TCGCSV as of October 15, 2026): base (main set 1–86) 86 cards $16.02, reverse holos 80 cards $21.09, secret rares 87 cards $2,887.99.

A master set is one copy of every card in the set, including the secret rares numbered past the printed set size, plus the reverse holo version of every card that has one. In Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution sets the ex, Ultra Rare and other higher-rarity cards don't come in reverse holo, so the reverse holo count (80) is lower than the main set count (86). Our master set price is the full set plus every reverse holo, at TCGPlayer market prices via TCGCSV. Some collectors also count promos or stamped versions. We don't include them.

White Flare FAQ

How much does it cost to complete White Flare?

About $2,904.01 for one copy of all 173 cards at TCGPlayer market prices. The main set (cards 1–86) is about $16.02. A master set with all 80 reverse holos is about $2,925.10.

How many cards are in White Flare?

White Flare has 173 cards: 86 in the main set (numbered 1–86) plus 87 numbered above 86.

What is the most expensive card in White Flare?

The most expensive White Flare card is Victini (#172, Rare) at about $550.89 market price.

How much does it cost to master set White Flare?

253 cards, about $2,925.10 at today's prices (TCGPlayer market prices via TCGCSV as of October 15, 2026): base (main set 1–86) 86 cards $16.02, reverse holos 80 cards $21.09, secret rares 87 cards $2,887.99. Most of it is the 87 secret rares, which add $2,887.99.

How many cards are in a White Flare master set?

253: 86 main-set cards, 87 secret rares and 80 reverse holos. 6 main-set cards have no reverse holo.

What counts toward a White Flare master set?

Every card numbered in the set, including secret rares past #86, plus the reverse holo of every card that has one. Promos and stamped versions aren't included in our total.

What's the cheapest way to master set White Flare?

Buy singles. The 86-card main set is about $16.02 and all 80 reverse holos about $21.09, so $37.11 covers everything except the secret rares. Buy the chase cards last.
