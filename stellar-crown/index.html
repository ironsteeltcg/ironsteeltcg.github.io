Stellar Crown FAQ

How much does it cost to complete Stellar Crown?

About $558.44 for one copy of all 175 cards at TCGPlayer market prices. The main set (cards 1–142) is about $41.89. A master set with all 125 reverse holos is about $596.11.

How many cards are in Stellar Crown?

Stellar Crown has 175 cards: 142 in the main set (numbered 1–142) plus 33 numbered above 142.

What is the most expensive card in Stellar Crown?

The most expensive Stellar Crown card is Squirtle (#148, Illustration Rare) at about $94.88 market price.
