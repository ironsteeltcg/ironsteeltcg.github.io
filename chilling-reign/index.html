Chilling Reign FAQ

How much does it cost to complete Chilling Reign?

About $2,016.26 for one copy of all 233 cards at TCGPlayer market prices. The main set (cards 1–198) is about $1,099.99. A master set with all 136 reverse holos is about $2,065.06.

How many cards are in Chilling Reign?

Chilling Reign has 233 cards: 198 in the main set (numbered 1–198) plus 35 numbered above 198.

What is the most expensive card in Chilling Reign?

The most expensive Chilling Reign card is Blaziken VMAX (#201, Rare Rainbow) at about $382.23 market price.
