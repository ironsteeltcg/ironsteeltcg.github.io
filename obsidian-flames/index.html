Obsidian Flames FAQ

How much does it cost to complete Obsidian Flames?

About $423.25 for one copy of all 230 cards at TCGPlayer market prices. The main set (cards 1–197) is about $58.32. A master set with all 176 reverse holos is about $475.12.

How many cards are in Obsidian Flames?

Obsidian Flames has 230 cards: 197 in the main set (numbered 1–197) plus 33 numbered above 197.

What is the most expensive card in Obsidian Flames?

The most expensive Obsidian Flames card is Charizard ex (#223, Special Illustration Rare) at about $94.27 market price.
