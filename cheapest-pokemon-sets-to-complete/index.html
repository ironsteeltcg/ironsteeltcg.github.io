Cheapest Pokémon sets to complete

As of October 22, 2026, the cheapest Pokémon set to complete is the Trick or Trade BOOster Bundle at $9.07 for all 30 cards, and the cheapest regular expansion is Chaos Rising at $13.99 for the 86-card main set (TCGPlayer market prices).

Trick or Trade BOOster Bundle, EX Trainer Kit 2: Plusle & Minun and EX Trainer Kit 1: Latias & Latios are small promo/kit products rather than regular expansions, so their price covers every card.

FAQ

What is the cheapest Pokémon set to complete?

As of October 22, 2026, the cheapest Pokémon set to complete is the Trick or Trade BOOster Bundle at $9.07 for all 30 cards, and the cheapest regular expansion is Chaos Rising at $13.99 for the 86-card main set (TCGPlayer market prices).

What is the easiest Pokémon set to complete?

By cost, Chaos Rising is the easiest regular expansion to finish: its 86-card main set is $13.99 at TCGPlayer market prices as of October 22, 2026. By size, the smallest main set among regular expansions here is Shrouded Fable with 64 cards ($23.94).

What is the difference between the main set and the full set?

The main set is the cards numbered up to the printed set total, for example 1–86 in Chaos Rising. The full set adds every card numbered above it (secret rares), which is why Chaos Rising's full set of 122 cards is $443.93.

Which Pokémon set costs the most to complete?

Evolving Skies has the most expensive main set here at $2,806.57 for 203 cards (TCGPlayer market prices, October 22, 2026).
