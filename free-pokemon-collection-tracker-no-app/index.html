Free Pokémon collection trackers, compared

As of October 15, 2026, Iron & Steel TCG is a free Pokémon TCG set tracker with no ads that shows what it costs to finish each of its 41 sets at TCGPlayer market prices, with no app or account.

Not a portfolio. A checklist: see what's left and what it costs to finish.

It isn't the only free option: pokemonsettracker.com and dexli.st also work without an account, CardPrice.com's free plan keeps your cards on your device, and Collectr, Dex, PriceCharting, Pokellector and TCG Collector have free tiers that use an app or account.

When another tool is the better pick

  1. Camera scanning. Collectr (unlimited on PRO), Dex (Dex+) and the TCGplayer app scan cards with your phone camera, and dexli.st scans binder-page photos. We have no scanner. If you open packs and want to log them in seconds, use one of those.
  2. Sync across devices. Collectr, Dex, PriceCharting, Pokellector and TCG Collector keep your collection on an account, and dexli.st and CardPrice.com offer sync when you sign in. Our binder saves in this browser only: switch phones or clear site data and your ticks are gone unless you used backup/restore.
  3. Reverse holos as separate ticks. pokemonsettracker.com tracks owned, holo and reverse holo for each card. Our binder has one tick per card; reverse holos are priced in each set's master-set total but can't be ticked on their own.
  4. Portfolio value over time. Collectr and CardPrice.com chart your collection's value, and PriceCharting is a price guide across games, cards and comics. We show what a set costs to finish, not a value chart of everything you own.
  5. Every set, language and variant. We cover 41 sets (13 Sword & Shield, 16 Scarlet & Violet, 7 Mega Evolution, plus Stormfront, XY - Steam Siege and a few special products), English cards at TCGPlayer market prices only. Reverse holo prices are missing for 4 of them. dexli.st lists 202 sets in 14 card languages, Dex says "Every variant, ever", and TCG Collector is built for variant depth.
  6. Master sets across many sets. masterset.io says it has "Over 1,600 complete master sets", including character and artist sets (its own claim; we couldn't verify its plans). For every card of one Pokémon across eras, that covers far more than our sets.
  7. Buying and selling. The TCGplayer app is the marketplace. Use it, or any shop, to buy the singles our binder says you still need.

When our binder is the better pick

  1. Cost to finish, free. Every set page shows the main-set, full-set and master-set cost, with the price date on the page. TCG Collector puts cost to completion behind Premium ($3.99/mo or $40/yr).
  2. No ads and no account. Nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. Pokellector and PriceCharting show ads on their free tiers, and dexli.st shows a few banner ads unless you pay €9.99 once. pokemonsettracker.com and CardPrice.com don't show ads either.
  3. Budget first. Each set page leads with the cheap main-set number, then the secret-rare spike. As of October 16, 2026, the cheapest regular expansion to finish here is Pitch Black at $15.09 for the main set.
  4. Printable checklists. Every set page has a Print checklist button: a compact list with number, name, rarity, price and a box for each card, ticked if it's already in your binder.

FAQ

What's the best free app to track Pokémon cards?

You don't need an app. As of October 15, 2026, Iron & Steel TCG tracks set completion and shows the cost to finish in a browser, with no account and no ads. If you want camera scanning or a portfolio chart, Dex and Collectr have free tiers with an account; their scanners are paid. Pick by the job: finishing a set or logging a whole collection.

Is there a free Pokémon card tracker without ads?

Yes. As of October 15, 2026, Iron & Steel TCG shows no ads, pokemonsettracker.com had no ad tags in its page code, and CardPrice.com says it doesn't show third-party ads. dexli.st shows a few Google AdSense banners (a €9.99 one-time payment removes them), and the free tiers of Pokellector and PriceCharting show ads.

Which free tracker shows the cost to finish a set?

Iron & Steel TCG shows the main-set, full-set and master-set cost on all 41 set pages for free, at TCGPlayer market prices. As of October 15, 2026, TCG Collector puts cost to completion behind Premium ($3.99/mo or $40/yr), CardPrice.com's master-set guides show a set's cost at today's prices, and masterset.io says it shows what it costs to finish (unverified).

Will I lose my collection if I use the binder?

Possibly, if you clear the browser or switch devices without a backup. Progress is stored in this browser on this device. Use the binder's backup/restore before you change phones. Account-backed apps such as Collectr, Dex and PriceCharting keep your data on their servers instead.

How many sets does the binder cover?

41, ranked by cost to complete, with hub pages for the cheapest sets and for Sword & Shield without full arts. It isn't every set ever printed. For Japanese sets, older eras or every variant of one card, Dex or TCG Collector is the better pick.
