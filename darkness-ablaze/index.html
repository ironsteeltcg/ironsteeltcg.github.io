How much is a Darkness Ablaze master set?

A Darkness Ablaze master set is 356 cards, $311.17 at today's prices (TCGPlayer market prices via TCGCSV as of October 15, 2026): base (main set 1–189) 189 cards $154.74, reverse holos 155 cards $68.73, secret rares 12 cards $87.70.

A master set is one copy of every card in the set, including the secret rares numbered past the printed set size, plus the reverse holo version of every card that has one. In Sword & Shield sets the V, VMAX, VSTAR and other higher-rarity cards don't come in reverse holo, so the reverse holo count (155) is lower than the main set count (189). Our master set price is the full set plus every reverse holo, at TCGPlayer market prices via TCGCSV. Some collectors also count promos or stamped versions. We don't include them.

Darkness Ablaze FAQ

How much does it cost to complete Darkness Ablaze?

About $242.44 for one copy of all 201 cards at TCGPlayer market prices. The main set (cards 1–189) is about $154.74. A master set with all 155 reverse holos is about $311.17.

How many cards are in Darkness Ablaze?

Darkness Ablaze has 201 cards: 189 in the main set (numbered 1–189) plus 12 numbered above 189.

What is the most expensive card in Darkness Ablaze?

The most expensive Darkness Ablaze card is Charizard VMAX (#020, Rare Holo VMAX) at about $38.80 market price.

How much does it cost to master set Darkness Ablaze?

356 cards, about $311.17 at today's prices (TCGPlayer market prices via TCGCSV as of October 15, 2026): base (main set 1–189) 189 cards $154.74, reverse holos 155 cards $68.73, secret rares 12 cards $87.70.

How many cards are in a Darkness Ablaze master set?

356: 189 main-set cards, 12 secret rares and 155 reverse holos. 34 main-set cards have no reverse holo.

What counts toward a Darkness Ablaze master set?

Every card numbered in the set, including secret rares past #189, plus the reverse holo of every card that has one. Promos and stamped versions aren't included in our total.

What's the cheapest way to master set Darkness Ablaze?

Buy singles. The 189-card main set is about $154.74 and all 155 reverse holos about $68.73, so $223.47 covers everything except the secret rares. Buy the chase cards last.
