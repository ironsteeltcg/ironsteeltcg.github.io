How much is a Surging Sparks master set?

A Surging Sparks master set is 417 cards, $1,180.94 at today's prices (TCGPlayer market prices via TCGCSV as of October 15, 2026): base (main set 1–191) 191 cards $54.12, reverse holos 165 cards $39.49, secret rares 61 cards $1,087.33.

A master set is one copy of every card in the set, including the secret rares numbered past the printed set size, plus the reverse holo version of every card that has one. In Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution sets the ex, Ultra Rare and other higher-rarity cards don't come in reverse holo, so the reverse holo count (165) is lower than the main set count (191). Our master set price is the full set plus every reverse holo, at TCGPlayer market prices via TCGCSV. Some collectors also count promos or stamped versions. We don't include them.

Surging Sparks FAQ

How much does it cost to complete Surging Sparks?

About $1,141.45 for one copy of all 252 cards at TCGPlayer market prices. The main set (cards 1–191) is about $54.12. A master set with all 165 reverse holos is about $1,180.94.

How many cards are in Surging Sparks?

Surging Sparks has 252 cards: 191 in the main set (numbered 1–191) plus 61 numbered above 191.

What is the most expensive card in Surging Sparks?

The most expensive Surging Sparks card is Pikachu ex (#238, Special Illustration Rare) at about $274.44 market price.

How much does it cost to master set Surging Sparks?

417 cards, about $1,180.94 at today's prices (TCGPlayer market prices via TCGCSV as of October 15, 2026): base (main set 1–191) 191 cards $54.12, reverse holos 165 cards $39.49, secret rares 61 cards $1,087.33. Most of it is the 61 secret rares, which add $1,087.33.

How many cards are in a Surging Sparks master set?

417: 191 main-set cards, 61 secret rares and 165 reverse holos. 26 main-set cards have no reverse holo.

What counts toward a Surging Sparks master set?

Every card numbered in the set, including secret rares past #191, plus the reverse holo of every card that has one. Promos and stamped versions aren't included in our total.

What's the cheapest way to master set Surging Sparks?

Buy singles. The 191-card main set is about $54.12 and all 165 reverse holos about $39.49, so $93.61 covers everything except the secret rares. Buy the chase cards last.
